HOW IT WORKS NOW:

WHEN YOU ENTER A CHECKPOINT:
1. The checkpoint box will light up - becoming lighter blue and more visible
2. "Checkpoint X" notification appears on screen
3. This checkpoint is now your respawn point

WHEN YOU GET HIT BY THE CAR:
- If you've hit a checkpoint: You respawn at that checkpoint
- If you haven't hit any checkpoint: You respawn at the game start position
- The car will kill you in BOTH cases
